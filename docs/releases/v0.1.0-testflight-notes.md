## What to Test

Thanks for testing SAMA Drum Practice! Here's what to focus on:

### Getting Started
1. Sign in with Apple (or tap "Skip" to use offline mode)
2. Select a curriculum item and tap **Start Practice**
3. Tap ostinatos, adjust tempo, log mistakes, and hit **Log Attempt**

### Key Flows to Try
- **Log a full session**: Pick a curriculum item, log a few attempts across different ostinatos, end the segment, then end the session
- **Review history**: Check the History tab to see your session with per-ostinato breakdowns
- **Browse sheet music**: Tap the Music tab and open any exercise
- **Check progress**: Visit the Progress tab to see mastery grid and tempo charts
- **Change settings**: Try switching metronome sounds, adjusting default tempo, and toggling haptics
- **Export data**: Go to Settings > Export Sessions (CSV) and share the file

### Feedback We're Looking For
- Does the practice logging feel fast and low-friction during a real session?
- Are touch targets large enough on iPad?
- Is anything confusing or hard to find?
- Does sync work when switching between devices?
- Any crashes or unexpected behavior?

### Known Issues
- App is iPad-optimized; iPhone layout may feel cramped
- Progress charts need several sessions of data before they're useful
- First sync after sign-in may take a moment if you have existing data

Please share feedback via TestFlight or message Buford directly.
